Start here

What it is
A yes, no or cannot trace answer to one question: can this customer take this amount on credit? It comes with a receipt that links the exact sales, payments and promises it used.
Who it is for
A small shop that sells on credit and keeps its books in a notebook or a spreadsheet.
Why a graph
Each sale, payment and promise is a record linked to its customer in FalkorDB, the graph database. The answer is a walk along those links, so the receipt can list the exact ones.
What to click
Press a button below. It opens a receipt in a few seconds. On the receipt, try the what-if on a record to see if the answer changes.

Check it yourself: the 255 test cases had their answers locked before Bashi ran (files in the repo). Try to tamper with a receipt and watch the check fail.

Phantom debt: the books still say a customer owes for a sale, but a payment of the same amount came in and was never linked to it. Inferred: a payment-to-sale link that a rule proposed, not one a person recorded. Every inferred link says so on the receipt.

Ways to use Bashi

  • This website. No sign-up. Use the buttons above.
  • MCP (a standard way for AI tools to call other tools): https://bashi-vrfv.onrender.com/mcp. Works in Cursor and other AI tools that take an MCP address.
  • Claude Desktop: a one-click extension, Bashi.mcpb.
  • Web API: /api/decide, /api/receipt, /api/verify, /api/whatif, /api/customers.
  • Guides an AI can read: /llms.txt and /AGENTS.md.
  • Your own shop: put your sales and payments in three CSV files (or three Google Sheet tabs published to the web) and load them into your own FalkorDB from the command line. It never touches this demo. Steps are in the README.
